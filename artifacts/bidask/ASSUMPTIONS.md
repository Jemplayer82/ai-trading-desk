# Card 65 / lane DF build assumptions

These are implementation choices for coordinator review; no deployment is authorized.

- Every new paper option position is bid_ask, regardless of account; the account fill_model denotes the current order default. Row-level fill_model preserves mid_legacy classification of existing positions; account.fill_model_cutover is the first upgrade timestamp in UTC (or new account creation timestamp). Closed position numbers and cash ledger history remain unchanged.
- On the first migration only, OPEN legacy positions reset liquidation mark and peak to recorded entry bid, discard the old midpoint-derived staged ratchet, and become stale until fresh quotes arrive. Existing cost basis remains unchanged. No pre-cutover bid peak history is available to reconstruct. This can alter future stop timing for the four open exported positions; coordinator must review before any deployment.
- A valid zero bid is an executable zero-dollar sale; a missing, negative, nonnumeric, nonfinite or implausibly large (>1e12) quote is unavailable. Buys require a positive ask and nonnegative bid with ask >= bid. Missing bids carry the last valid bid with a stale flag; if none exists, exclude the sell. Ask/mid are never substitutes.
- Entry bid zero is permitted. Fixed percentage stops have a zero reference and no positive trigger; trailing policies can act once a positive bid peak exists. A staged policy tightens after any positive rise from the zero reference.
- Stop-limit orders retain trigger/limit eligibility, but fills execute at the observed bid. Stops are recorded at the decision timestamp; no inferred historical trigger-price fills or backdating.
- Expiry follows the explicitly requested paper liquidation policy: sell at the last valid bid, flag stale, record reason expiry and status closed. This does not model physical exercise or cash intrinsic settlement. Historical expired rows remain unchanged. Summary keys settled_itm/expired_worthless remain compatible but count positive/zero expiry liquidation respectively.
- There is no commission model in the desk option cash helpers; the existing premium-only accounting is retained. Re-score preserves implied recorded entry/exit fees.
- Real-data smoke uses a disposable SQLite database seeded from all 96 exported positions. No source desk DB copy was supplied or discovered. It exercises real recorded entry bid/ask and contracts, supplemental zero/missing scenarios and every stop policy/exit reason, then discards numeric values. Historical exit quotes are unavailable, so this cannot validate unrecorded exit bid/ask. No provider/LLM network call is made by smoke.
- Re-score is a paired fill sensitivity estimate, keeping recorded selections, counts and exit times. Exit relative spread is the recorded entry spread divided by entry bid/ask midpoint, applied to recorded exit premium as the exit-midpoint estimate for all closed rows. A separate sensitivity preserves recorded cash settlement proceeds. It is not an ask-sizing or bid-stop replay.
